# Surface brief: public site (Home, Category, Search, Project, Error)

Scope and mode: public catalog; admin out of scope and must not change. Treated as Persuade-leaning Operate: browse by category leads.
Audience/job: developers (and agents) judging whether a project is alive and seeing it in use.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The catalog is a step sequencer. Every project is a track of sixteen step keys; lit keys equal score divided by five. Refuses the SaaS card grid and the generic dark dev-tool skin.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black ultramarine night panel (#090828, deeper #04031a). Four lit quarters: pink, salmon, yellow, mint. Score numeral takes the colour of its last lit key. Unbounded for headings, Anybody caps for labels and keys, Sometype Mono for readouts, Hanken Grotesk for reading text. Physical keys: bottom-edge inset shadow, press moves 2px.

STORY: The visitor reads the tempo (top project score), picks a bank (category), scans tracks by how far the keys light, and opens one. They trust the score because its parts are shown as more step rows.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left panel: giant mint readout of the top score, title, lead, search, tier legend as mini step rows. Right: category banks as keys, then Most actively maintained as tracks with a running chase light.

FORM: Drum machine step row, user-chosen from a bolder-register roll, palette B (ultramarine night). seed:1d0fb3329108723f. Signature move: the 16-key row; on the project page it lights in sequence once.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
